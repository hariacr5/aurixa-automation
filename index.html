FAQ

Do I need UiPath to work with Aurixa?

No. The right technology depends on the process. We can evaluate RPA, workflow tools, APIs, data automation and AI-based approaches.

Can a small business benefit from automation?

Yes. A good starting point is one repetitive process with enough volume or effort to justify automation.

How do we identify a process to automate?

Start with a process that is repetitive, rules-driven, time-consuming, error-prone or involves moving data between systems.
