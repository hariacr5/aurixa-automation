FAQ

Questions before you start

Do I need UiPath to work with Aurixa?

No. We select the technology based on the process. Options can include RPA, workflow tools, APIs, data automation and AI.

Can a small business benefit from automation?

Yes. A good starting point is one repetitive process with enough volume or effort to justify automation.

How do we find a process to automate?

Look for work that is repetitive, rules-driven, time-consuming, error-prone or involves moving data between systems.

What happens in the free assessment?

We discuss one process, understand the current steps and identify whether automation is worth exploring. There is no obligation to proceed.
